Mechanism 2 -- TSAN dl_iterate_phdr interceptor <-> dynamic-loader-lock inversion.

Captured with `gdb -p <pid> -batch -ex "thread apply all bt"` while the reproducer
(src/dlopen_loi.rs) was in a throughput collapse under ThreadSanitizer on
aarch64 / RHEL9 glibc / rustc nightly-2026-05-19. These stacks are verbatim from
the running process; frame paths say `src/main.rs` because the scratchpad
reproducer crate used that filename -- `actor_backtrace` / `actor_dlopen` are the
same functions committed here as src/dlopen_loi.rs.

Two locks:
  L = glibc dynamic-loader lock (taken by dl_iterate_phdr AND dlopen/dlclose)
  T = TSAN internal LibIgnore lock (sanitizer_mutex, taken by TSAN's interceptors)

    calls _Unwind_Find_FDE -> dl_iterate_phdr (loader lock L) per frame.

(C) DLOPEN side, blocked WANTING T. Another dlclose thread is parked acquiring

================================================================================
Cycle: capture threads sit in _Unwind_Find_FDE/dl_iterate_phdr holding/queuing on
L while TSAN wants T; dlclose threads hold T (OnLibraryLoaded) and call
dl_iterate_phdr wanting L. L<->T taken in opposite orders across threads ->
lock-order inversion. Observed effect: capture throughput collapsed from
~32,000 per 500ms to 0-7 per 500ms (momentary hard 0) for a sustained window.
On glibc with a recursive loader lock this is a severe livelock; on a
non-recursive loader path it is a permanent hang. Either way: denial of service.
